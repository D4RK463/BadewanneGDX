The pool attendant just wanted to take a leisurely bath and then relax with his talking rubber duck for the rest of the day.
But suddenly they both get sucked down the drain and find themselves in the room of a child.

Why are they here? How do they get out of the room? And above all, who locked them in there?

These and other questions have to be answered in this point and click adventure, with witty dialog,
inane puzzles and unusual characters. Have fun!
